Things she’ll notice

“Picture it:”

Every glance starts like one of Sophia’s stories. Picture it: 10:42, Miami. The digits are cast in the girl’s own metal: rose gold, platinum, pearl or gold.

The arch keeps the seconds

Two lines climb the window’s trim from either side and meet at the keystone at the top of every minute. The little sunburst flashes when they touch.

A countdown to cheesecake

The bottom line counts down to midnight. From 12:00 to 12:59 AM it simply reads It’s cheesecake o’clock. Try the midnight button above.

Four girls, four nights

Each version has its own sky, metal and keepsake on the table: Blanche’s magnolia under a full moon, Dorothy’s coffee by a crescent, Rose’s daisies at sunrise, Sophia’s wicker purse at sunset.

The four wallpapers

Full-size PNGs are in golden-girls-watchface/wallpapers/: the 410×502 set for Ultra and Ultra 2, and 422×514 for Ultra 3. The sky up top is left open on purpose, because that is where watchOS puts the time.

Put it on her wrist

  1. Save the four PNGs to her iPhone’s Photos and add them to a new album called Golden Girls.

  2. Open the Watch app on her iPhone, go to Face Gallery and tap Photos.

  3. Under the content options, choose the four photos or point it at the Golden Girls album.

  4. Tap Add. A different girl shows up each time she raises her wrist or taps the screen.

Good to know: Apple doesn’t allow custom animated watch faces, so on the watch the time is drawn by watchOS in Apple’s own font, over the open sky. The climbing arch, the “Picture it:” lettering and the cheesecake countdown are the live concept you see above.
