WHAT'S ON THE FACE

BEZEL
60 minute ticks, gold cardinal markers, a sweeping seconds trail with a comet head, and two analog chevrons: gold for the hour, theme color for the minute.
GAUGES
Four segmented arcs: PWR battery (turns red at 15%, breathes while charging), ♥ BPM heart rate (the label pulses at your measured rate), STEPS toward your goal, and CORE battery temperature in °C or °F to match your weather unit.
CARDINALS
J.A.R.V.I.S tag at 12, week number at 3, live seconds at 6, unread messages at 9.
REACTOR
Counter-rotating dashed and segmented rings, ten coil notches, a radar sweep and a hex grid that shifts with wrist tilt.
CORE
Weather and condition, a greeting that changes through the day, a glowing clock that follows your 12/24-hour setting, the date, a telemetry ticker that cycles every 3 s (high/low, rain, UV, lunar phase, day and week, messages, step goal), and a status line that flags charging, critical power, high heart rate or a finished step goal.
PODS
Two customizable complication slots. They default to sunrise/sunset and your next event. The simulator fills them with sample data.
SECOND ZONE
Zulu, Malibu, New York, London, Dubai, Tokyo or Sydney.
TAPS
Battery gauge → battery, heart gauge → heart rate, time → alarms, date → calendar, status → messages, core gauge → settings, J.A.R.V.I.S tag → music player.
WAKE
A sonar ping sweeps the face whenever it becomes visible, and again when you tap empty space.
STANDBY
Always-on mode drops to the time, date, chevrons, dim ticks and power level to save battery.

INSTALL IT

  1. Get JarvisHUD.apk onto a computer. In the source it lives at jarvis-watchface/release/.
  2. On the watch: Settings → About watch → Software → tap Software version 5 times. Then Settings → Developer options → turn on ADB debugging and Wireless debugging, and tap Pair new device.
  3. From a computer on the same Wi-Fi: 
    adb pair <ip>:<pairing-port>
adb connect <ip>:<port>
adb install JarvisHUD.apk
    No computer? Phone apps such as Bugjaeger can push the APK over the same wireless debugging link.
  4. Long-press the current watch face, scroll to J.A.R.V.I.S. HUD, then tap Customize to pick the suit color, title, second time zone and the two pods.
