EYES
Angry glowing slits. They blink, narrow as your heart rate climbs, flare while charging, and drift with your wrist tilt. Tap them to fire eye lasers.
CLOCK
A huge glowing clock with red and cyan chromatic fringes. Glitch intensity: Off, Menacing (a short glitch every 12 s) or Unhinged (jitter, torn slices and CRT scanlines every few seconds).
READOUT
Steps, outside temperature and unread messages on one line under the date. The mouth grille below it thrums with every heartbeat.
ECG
A sweep monitor that moves at your measured heart rate, and flatlines in red when there's no reading.
LEGION
12 drones orbit the bezel. One comes online for every twelfth of your step goal, and the right gauge tracks the same progress.
CORE
Battery on the left gauge. It flashes red at 15% and breathes in metal colors while charging.
BEZEL
A spinning sawblade, a counter-rotating gear ring, a seconds spark, a steel hour blade and a red minute blade.
DOOM
EXTINCTION T-minus countdown to midnight, a red purge flash at the top of every minute, and a heartbeat-synced red vignette.
WAKE
Ignition sequence: eye flares and a jagged shockwave every time the face becomes visible.
PODS
Two complication slots, defaulting to sunrise/sunset and your next event. The simulator fills them with sample data.
TAPS
Eyes → lasers, ULTRON tag → music, time → alarms, date → calendar, readout → messages, ECG → heart rate, core gauge → battery, countdown → settings.
DORMANT
Always-on keeps the dim eyes, the time, the blades and the battery level.

INSTALL

  1. Get Ultron.apk onto your computer. In the source it lives at ultron-watchface/release/.
  2. On the watch, turn on ADB debugging and Wireless debugging in Developer options, then connect with adb connect <ip>:<port>.
  3. If adb devices lists your phone too, name the watch with -s: 
    adb -s <ip>:<port> install "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\Ultron.apk"
  4. Long-press the watch face, add ULTRON, then tap Customize for core color (Vibranium by default), glitch intensity, the drones and the two pods.
